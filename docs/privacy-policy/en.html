Privacy Policy

Free Parking Finder — Finland · Effective 21 August 2026

This policy explains what data the Free Parking Finder — Finland app ("the app", "we", "us") collects, why, and how you can control it. The app is developed by an individual developer, Yomal Fernando, based in Finland.

1. Data we collect

DataWhenWhy
Precise device location (GPS) While the app is open and in use To show parking spots near you and to tag the location when you submit a report. We do not collect location in the background.
Account identifier On first launch (guest) or when you create an account Guests get an anonymous device-linked identifier automatically. If you register, we also store the email address you provide. We never see your password — it's handled entirely by Firebase Authentication.
Parking spot reports When you submit or correct a report The GPS coordinates, the interpreted parking rule, a timestamp, and your account identifier are stored so the spot can be shown to other users and the rule can be re-verified over time.
Sign photos When you photograph a parking sign The photo is sent directly to Google's Gemini AI model to read the rules on the sign. It is never uploaded to our own servers or stored anywhere — it's deleted from your device as soon as the screen where you took it is closed, whether or not you submit the report.
Advertising identifiers If you're using the app as a guest Guest users see ads served through Google AdMob, which may use device advertising identifiers per Google's own advertising policies. Registered users see fewer ads and can remove full-screen ads entirely.
Device attestation signals On every request to our backend Firebase App Check verifies that requests come from a genuine, unmodified copy of the app, to prevent abuse. This doesn't build a profile of you.

2. Who we share data with

We don't sell your data. We use the following service providers to run the app:

  • Google Firebase — authentication, database (Firestore), app integrity checks (App Check), and AI photo interpretation (Gemini via Firebase AI Logic).
  • Google AdMob — serves ads to guest users. See Google's advertising policy.
  • OpenStreetMap — supplies the map tiles you see. Loading a map tile sends your device's IP address and the visible map area to OpenStreetMap's tile servers, the same as any map website.

3. How long we keep data

  • Parking spot reports (location + rule) are kept indefinitely as part of the shared community map, since they describe a place rather than a person. A spot's "reported free/full right now" status automatically expires after 30 minutes, though the underlying spot record stays.
  • Correction proposals (reports that a spot's rules are wrong) are cleared automatically once enough people agree and the correction is applied.
  • Sign photos are never stored — see above.
  • Account data is kept until you ask us to delete it.

4. Your rights

If you're in the EU/EEA, UK, or another jurisdiction with similar protections, you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or export your personal data, and to object to how it's processed. The app doesn't yet have a self-service delete-my-account button — email us at yomalfdo@gmail.com and we'll handle the request within 30 days.

5. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

6. Security

We rely on Firebase's security infrastructure (encrypted connections, App Check, authenticated access) to protect your data. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. Updates will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Email yomalfdo@gmail.com.
